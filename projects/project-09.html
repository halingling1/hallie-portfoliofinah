The idea

This project was about first creating GIFs through Photoshop, either hand-drawn or using videos. Next there was research needed about GIFs to enhance this website. Through this assignment, we learned how to create divisions and colored sections.

Process

Through my research, I learned that GIFs are made from a series of frames that play in a loop and can show movement without sound. I created my GIFs in Photoshop by arranging drawn or video-based frames, setting their timing, and making sure they loop smoothly. I then organized the website into different divisions with colored sections for the history, examples, information, and key takeaways. I used headings, images, and spacing to make the research easier to follow.

What I learned

I learned how to create and edit GIFs in Photoshop and how to structure a webpage with different sections. Next time, I would like to explore more advanced techniques for creating smooth animations and improving the overall design of the page.
