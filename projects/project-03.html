Girl in Red (Perfect Blue inspired)

Art 75 · Video · Video with sound and lighting editing

The idea

This video is inspired by Perfect Blue, in which the main character is followed by a doppelgänger of her old idol self that she can no longer separate from who she really is. I wanted to take that idea of a person confronted by another version of herself and turn it into a short, tense scene. The piece is about how identity can turn against itself.

Process

I used a camera to film the scenes, focusing on close-up shots and changes in lighting to make the doppelgänger feel separate from the original character. In the edit, I used a slower beginning before cutting more quickly during the attack to make the scene feel more intense. One problem I solved was making the doppelgänger look similar to the original character while still making her feel threatening, so I used the red clothing, umbrella, and lighting to separate her from the first character.

What I learned

I learned how changes in lighting, color, and pacing can completely change the mood of a scene. Next time, I would spend more time refining the attack sequence and adding more sound effects to make the ending feel more dramatic.
