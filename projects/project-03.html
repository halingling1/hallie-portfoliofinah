The idea

I filmed the scenes by focusing on close-up shots and changes in lighting to make the doppelgänger feel separate from the original character. In the edit, I used a slower beginning before cutting more quickly during the attack to make the scene feel more intense. One problem I solved was making the doppelgänger look similar to the original character while still making her feel threatening, so I used the red clothing, umbrella, and lighting to separate her from the first character.

Process

I used a camera to film the scenes and focused on close-up shots and changes in lighting. In the edit, I used a slower beginning before cutting more quickly during the attack to make the scene feel more intense. One problem I solved was making the doppelgänger look similar to the original character while still making her feel threatening, so I used the red clothing, umbrella, and lighting to separate her from the first character.

What I learned

I learned how changes in lighting, color, and pacing can completely change the mood of a scene. Next time, I would spend more time refining the attack sequence and adding more sound effects to make the ending feel more dramatic.
