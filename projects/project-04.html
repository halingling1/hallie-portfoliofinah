Jump Scare

Art 75 · Video and sound editing

Watch on YouTube

The idea

This project was to develop my skills in video editing along with sound. The video is short, yet impactful in the horror essence, and is very unsettling and suspenseful for an audience.

Process

I built the suspense by using slow pacing, unexpected cuts, and unsettling sound effects. I allowed certain moments to stay quiet before adding louder sounds to make the sudden changes feel more intense. One problem I solved was making the sound match the timing of the visuals so the video felt more suspenseful instead of confusing.

What I learned

I learned how to effectively use sound and editing to create tension and fear in a short video. Next time, I would experiment with different types of sound effects and timing to make the jump scare even more effective.
