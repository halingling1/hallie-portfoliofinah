Sticker Project

Art 74 · Illustrator and compositing

The idea

This project was about creating a 3D still life object in Procreate and turning it into a sticker. I designed a small light-green character and tried to give it depth through its rounded shape, shading, and shadow. I then placed the sticker onto a photo of my laptop to show how it could look on a real-life object.

Process

I used different layers and soft brushes in Procreate to build the character’s shape, highlights, shading, and shadow. I added a white sticker border and placed the design into a photo of my laptop, adjusting its size and position to make it look like it belonged with the other stickers. One problem I solved was making the character look three-dimensional instead of flat, so I used shading and highlights to create more depth.

What I learned

I learned how lighting, shadows, and highlights can make a simple drawing look more three-dimensional. Next time, I would spend more time refining the shading and proportions so the object looks even more realistic.
