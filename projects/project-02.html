Chibi Character

ART 102 · 3D modeling in Blender

The idea

A chibi character I modeled in Blender for ART 102: a small blue figure with a sprout on its head, a bandage on its forehead, and a spiral shirt, lit with soft pink and blue light.

Process

I began by blocking out the character’s head, body, limbs, and clothing with simple shapes. I then refined the forms through smoothing and sculpting before adding the eyes, sprout, bandage, spiral shirt design, and other small details. I created materials for each part of the character and used pink and blue lights to add contrast and make the character’s features stand out. One problem I solved was keeping the character’s details visible while maintaining its smooth, rounded chibi style.

What I learned

I learned how much lighting can affect the mood and readability of a 3D character. Next time, I would spend more time refining the shirt design and adding more texture to the clothing and other materials.
