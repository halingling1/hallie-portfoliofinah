Album Cover

Art 74 · Photoshop and glitch art

The idea

This project was about creating a glitch-based album cover for the band Wave to Earth. I wanted the design to match the band’s calm and atmospheric style while showing the imperfect feeling of corrupted digital images. I used a photograph of someone watching a screen and distorted it with glitch effects, layering, and fragmented text to create a nostalgic and dreamlike mood. The final design was meant to feel like an old memory or recording being replayed.

Process

I created the glitch images by opening the image data in TextEdit and changing parts of the text to distort the original image. I then brought the glitched images into Photoshop and layered them with the original image, adjusting the opacity, placement, and colors to create the album cover. One problem I solved was keeping the main image recognizable while adding enough distortion to make the glitch effect noticeable.

What I learned

I learned that mistakes and corrupted image data can be used as intentional design elements. Next time, I would experiment with more ways to control the glitching process so the final image feels more planned and consistent.
