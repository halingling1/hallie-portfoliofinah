After the Dance

Art 3 · Video and animation · 2026

The idea

After the Dance is a short video and animation piece about moving on from something that shaped a big part of my identity. I was in lion dance for about six years, and I made this to show that stepping away from it doesn't mean forgetting it. I mixed old lion dance footage with animated sections I drew in Procreate, so the footage feels like documented memory and the animation feels like my own reflection. The red and gold of the performances contrast with darker, quieter tones, and at the end an animated version of me walks away from the lion dance imagery.

Process

I animated the reflective sections in Procreate and edited them together with the footage using layered, blended transitions and slower pacing, so the piece feels like revisiting a memory instead of a straight story.

What I learned

I learned how animation and documentary footage can work together to show both a real experience and an emotional response to it. Next time, I would spend more time refining the transitions so the shift between the footage and the animation feels even smoother.
